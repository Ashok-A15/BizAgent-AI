Mysuru Wellness Clinic is a premier healthcare facility located in Gokulam, Mysuru, dedicated to providing comprehensive family medicine and specialized diagnostic care. Established in 2018, the clinic serves local residents and visiting professionals with modern medical equipment, an in-house laboratory, and experienced physicians across general medicine, pediatrics, and preventive cardiology.

The clinic offers outpatient consultations from Monday to Saturday between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM, and on Sundays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM for urgent consultations. Specialized services include diabetic care management, geriatric health evaluations, childhood immunizations, executive health checkups, and minor surgical procedures performed in an accredited day-care setup.

Patients can schedule prior appointments via phone or through the clinic digital portal to minimize waiting times. Walk-in emergencies are attended to by duty doctors immediately. The clinic also provides home sample collection for diagnostic lab tests across Vijayanagar, Jayalakshmipuram, and Saraswathipuram areas in Mysuru, delivering digital reports within four hours.
